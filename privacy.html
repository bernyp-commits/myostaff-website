Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

1. Who we are

My-O-Staff Pty Ltd (“My-O-Staff”, “we”, “us”) operates this website (myostaff.com). We are committed to protecting your personal information in accordance with the Australian Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs). For any privacy questions, contact us at support@myostaff.com.

2. Information we collect

We collect only the information you choose to give us:

  • Contact and booking details — when you submit our contact form or book a call, we receive your name, email address, phone number and any message you include. Form submissions are processed by our CRM provider and used solely to respond to your enquiry.
  • Calendly bookings — when you book a meeting, Calendly collects the details you provide under its own privacy policy.
  • Correspondence — if you email us, we keep that correspondence to handle your request.

We do not collect sensitive information, and we never ask you to upload company data through this website.

3. How we use information

  • To respond to enquiries and provide our services;
  • To schedule and manage appointments;
  • To send information you have requested (such as a referral partner info pack);
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not send marketing emails unless you have opted in.

4. Third-party services

This website embeds content and tools from third parties, each governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Calendly — appointment booking;
  • Close (CRM) — contact form processing;
  • YouTube, Vimeo and Facebook — embedded videos;
  • Cloudflare — website hosting, security and performance. Cloudflare may set a security cookie (such as the one behind /cdn-cgi/ URLs) to protect this site from bots.

When you click a link to an external site (including the program operator’s site, Udemy, LinkedIn and goplasticneutral.com), their privacy policies apply from that point.

5. Referral program disclosure

My-O-Staff is an independent referral partner of the data-licensing program described on this website. If you complete a data licensing transaction with the program operator after being referred by us, the operator pays My-O-Staff a referral fee. This fee is paid by the operator and is never deducted from any payment made to you. See our Terms & Conditions and the disclosure on our Data Opportunity page.

6. Data security and retention

Information is stored with reputable service providers using industry-standard security. We retain personal information only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law. Website hosting is provided by Cloudflare over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

7. Your rights

You may request access to the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by emailing support@myostaff.com. We will respond within a reasonable time. If you are not satisfied with our handling of your information, you may complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at oaic.gov.au.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is always available on this page.
